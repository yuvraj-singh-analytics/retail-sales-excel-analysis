# 📊 Retail Sales & Operations Advanced Excel Analysis

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project performs an in-depth data analysis of retail operations using an advanced Excel workbook. It processes nearly 10,000 transaction records to evaluate sales performance, profit margins, shipping modes, and regional profitability.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
* **Spreadsheet Software:** Microsoft Excel
* **Functions & Formulas:** `XLOOKUP`, `SUMIFS`, nested logical formulas, Data Validation.
* **Data Modeling & Summarization:** Pivot Tables, Slicers, Calculated Fields, and KPI Dashboards.

## 📈 Key Business Questions Addressed
1. **Customer Behavior:** Identified the most utilized shipping modes (Standard, Second, First Class) and top-tier customers by order frequency and sales volume.
2. **Product Profitability:** Analyzed category and sub-category margins (Technology vs. Office Supplies vs. Furniture) to pinpoint high-yield product lines.
3. **Operations & Logistics:** Assessed shipping cost structures per state and return rates to optimize supply chain visibility.

## 🖼️ Dashboards & Visuals
* **Sales Dashboard:** Overview of regional sales contributions (Managed by regional leads Anna Andreadi, Chuck Magee, Kelly Williams, and Cassandra Brandow).
* **KPIs & Distribution Dashboards:** High-level executive summary cards tracking total revenue, net profit, and COGS.
